## MaliPDF 1.0.3

MaliPDF is now on the **Mac App Store** and the **Microsoft Store**; both keep it up to date automatically. A signed DMG for Apple Silicon Macs is attached below for direct download.

### New
- **Customizable toolbars** in Focus Mode and presentations: add or remove tools with the Customize button.
- **Presentations**: shapes (line, arrow, rectangle, ellipse) and text; tools use the same colours and settings as the main toolbar; hold Shift for squares, circles and 45° lines.
- **Double-click a tool** in the Focus Mode toolbar to open its settings next to it.
- Focus Mode and presentations **fill the screen**; in full screen the tab bar hides and slides in at the top edge, and Esc leaves full screen.

### Fixed
- Saving a PDF in the Mac App Store version failed with "operation not permitted".
- A page could stay on a grey placeholder after fast scrolling.
- Smaller download: unused files are no longer packed.
