## MaliPDF 1.4.0

MaliPDF **1.4** unifies Mac, iPad, and iPhone on a single milestone release across the **Mac App Store**, **App Store (Universal iPhone & iPad)**, **Microsoft Store**, and direct Apple Silicon `.dmg` download.

### New
- **Mac opens Library first**: Launching MaliPDF on macOS now opens your **Library & Cloud** home screen first, then transitions seamlessly into the full multi-tab desktop PDF workspace when you open or create a document.
- **Universal iPhone + iPad App (`1.4`)**: MaliPDF on iOS is now a Universal build for both iPhone and iPad, featuring one-finger natural drawing on iPhone, quick Hand/Pan tool switching, and compact Dynamic Island–ready toolbars and sheets.
- **Hardened iCloud Drive & Cloud Sync**: Full handling for evicted macOS `.icloud` placeholders, persistent Mac App Store security-scoped folder bookmarks, conflict-safe dual copies, and zero-race native iOS iCloud Drive startup.
- **120 Hz Live Ink**: Incremental wet/dry stroke rendering keeps long handwriting strokes fluid on ProMotion displays.
