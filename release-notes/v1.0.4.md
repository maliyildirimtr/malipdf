## MaliPDF 1.0.4

MaliPDF is on the **Mac App Store** and the **Microsoft Store**; both keep it up to date automatically. A DMG for Apple Silicon Macs is attached below for direct download.

### New
- **The same keyboard shortcut for each tool in every mode** — normal view, Focus Mode and presentations: P pen, M highlighter, E eraser, T text, L line, A arrow, R rectangle, C ellipse, F polygon, K laser pointer, V select.
- **Select tool in presentations**: click ink you drew to move it or delete it.

### Fixed
- **Eraser**: one touch removes the whole stroke (as in presentations). The line-cutting "Stroke Eraser" is still available in the toolbar.
